Field Transect Lab Instructions

Welcome to the Virtual Belt Transect Lab! In ecological field studies, sampling along a line transect allows biologists to estimate population density and analyze spatial distribution patterns of species.

Transect Dimensions:
  • Transect Length: 50 meters (0 m to 50 m)
  • Transect Width: 1 meter on the left (-1 m) + 1 meter on the right (+1 m) = 2 meters total width.
  • Total Sampling Area: 50 m × 2 m = 100 m².

Lab Instructions:

  1. Select the Morning (07:00) sampling period.
  2. Pan along the 50 m transect line using the navigation controls or slider to visually count all individuals of Organism 1 (Red), Organism 2 (Blue), and Organism 3 (Yellow).
  3. Enter your recorded totals into the Sampling Data Table or click "Record Current Sample".
  4. Repeat the procedure for Afternoon (13:00) and Evening (20:00).
  5. Observe how time of day affects species counts and spatial clustering.

Spatial Distribution Patterns:

Clumped Individuals aggregate in patches or groups near resources.
Uniform Individuals maintain equal spacing due to competition/territoriality.
Random Location of each individual is independent of others.
